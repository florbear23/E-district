About

Created by entrepreneurs, for entrepreneurs.

E-District exists because its founders wanted the building they never had.

The idea

A launchpad, not a landlord.

From high-speed internet to on-site marketing solutions and billboard advertising that reaches millions of drivers every month, E-District is built to be the place where entrepreneurs join a creative atmosphere — where start-ups collaborate to facilitate growth and establish the success of their business.

Dare to dream bigger, bolder and brighter. E-District is the launchpad for those who chase ambitions and crave a community that understands their same passion.

What that means in practice

Three things the building is built around.

01

One roof, every resource

Fiber internet, meeting and presentation rooms, a full-service cafeteria and an on-site marketing agency. The things a growing company would otherwise assemble from five vendors are simply part of the building.

02

A community that stays involved

Tenants describe E-District as engaged rather than absentee — a working community with shared space designed for the people in it, not just a corridor of locked doors.

03

A location that does work for you

Ten minutes from downtown Salt Lake City, on a corridor carrying millions of vehicles a month, with E-District's own digital billboard above it.
